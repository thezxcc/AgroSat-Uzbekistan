# AgroSat Uzbekistan — independent farmer usability evidence protocol
Date: 2026-11-15. Status: **NOT PERFORMED** until real participants use the product.
Purpose: measure the actual comprehension and practical usability of the farmer-first interface, without inventing agricultural results.

## Pilot sample and ethics
Ask 3–5 adult volunteers who are not AgroSat developers; preferably at least one person with practical farming experience or familiarity with irrigation. Participation is voluntary. Do not collect names, phone numbers, photos, audio, GPS, farm ownership documents or private financial data. Use hypothetical example fields; don't issue real-world planting instructions.

## Script (one tester, 5–8 minutes)
Give the tester the app without explaining its controls. Ask them to choose a language they understand.
Scenario 1: "Your field has no summer irrigation, and you don't know your soil salinity." Can they enter the answers without help, reach the result, and explain in their own words why an extra summer crop is risky?
Scenario 2: "You have only a little irrigation available. What should you verify next?" Check that they notice the **limited water: irrigation risk** status and do not mistake a data warning for a promise of harvest.
Scenario 3: "Where could you read which NASA and research sources were used?" Ask them to find the evidence or scientific details without guidance.
Ask one final question: "What should a farmer do before choosing an option from this site?" Correct examples: check water delivery and timing, soil measurements and crop calendar; NOT "plant A automatically".

## Honest quantitative measures (calculate ONLY after real tests)
Task completion rate = testers who completed / actual testers.
Risk comprehension = testers who correctly explained the water warning / actual testers.
Next-action recognition = testers who identified a real next check / actual testers.
NASA evidence discovery = testers who opened the source trace / actual testers.
These are **interface usefulness** measurements, not measured crop yield, soil recovery, irrigation savings or farm adoption.

## Reporting
Until testing occurs, Project Tab may truthfully say: "Designed for farmer comprehension and validated with automated accessibility/responsive tests; field usability trials and agronomic outcome validation remain future work."
After real testing: describe participant count, date, conditions, results and failures. Do not claim evidence not observed.
